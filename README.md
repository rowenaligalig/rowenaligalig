# About me 🌲

I am a PhD candidate in Environmental Sciences at the Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM). My research examines how natural disturbances and forest management affect carbon dynamics in Québec mixedwood forests.

Using **LANDIS-II**, a forest landscape model, I simulate how spruce budworm, wildfire, wind, climate change, and management interact to shape long-term forest structure, composition, and carbon dynamics across large landscapes.
